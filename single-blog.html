lore

Fate of Antara: The Book of Elements

Join Discord

In Antara, four elemental ant nations—Fire, Earth, Water, and Air—were once guided by the legendary Book of Elements. When Norbakh shattered that balance, the world fell into chaos.

Follow a story-driven single-player journey across forests, mountains, caverns, and skies as you master ant-inspired powers, uncover hidden paths, and fight to restore unity to the realm.
