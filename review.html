Game Overview

A story-driven single-player RPG where four elemental ant nations fight for the fate of Antara.

Story Driven Fantasy RPG

Save the World of Antara

Fate of Antara is a cinematic story-based single-player RPG set in a fantasy world ruled by powerful elemental ant nations.

The peaceful lands of Antara are falling into darkness as Norbakh rises to conquer the world and seize the legendary Oron Stone — the ancient source of balance and elemental power.

Players must complete missions, explore dangerous regions, fight powerful enemies, and reclaim lands captured by darkness. Each nation offers unique elemental abilities, combat styles, and strategic powers that must be mastered to survive.

Travel nation by nation through Fire, Earth, Water, and Air kingdoms, unlocking their strengths and discovering hidden secrets of the Book of Elements while preparing for the final battle against Norbakh.
