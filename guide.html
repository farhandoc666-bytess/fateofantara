    Road Map / Guide

    Main Menu

    • On start of game you need to sign in through your Steam account, so internet should be good. About 20 MB is enough.
    • One time you need to press Steam sign in. Next time you will auto-login until you install Windows again.

    Message Bar

    Bottom message from Game Developers for all users, so keep it secret. It is only for users who are playing this game.

    Character Setup

    • Every nation has individual characteristics and an individual profile card.
    • Levels and rank are separate for each nation.
    • You have to upgrade them individually and progress their level separately.
    • Even on the leaderboard it will show separately.
    • Select your nation in character setup.
    • Upgrade your character characteristics through the character panel or view it.
    • For gadgets add or upgrade you need to go to the shop from the bottom shop button in the main menu.
    • The abilities section is under review and will be updated ASAP.

    Profile Card

    It shows your ant level, progress, score and kills with your username.

    It is different for each ant nation.

    Continue Button

    All settings will be saved when you press Continue and you will return to the main menu.

    So avoid using go back after changing cloth, gadgets and other items.

    Characteristics Panel

    This panel explains how much values you are on your ant. Each nation is separately upgraded and applied.

    You can buy or upgrade each characteristic value by pressing the upgrade button and using AQ and FOA coins.

    Road Map

    Shows mission details according to your progress and shows all islands and planets.

    Shop

    This is the section where you can add different items, rides and consumable items using your AQ and FOA coins.

    In coins section you can purchase AQ and FOA coins.

    Reward Panel

    • On each day login you get rewarded by some items or some coins. It is totally random.
    • Do not forget to get your daily rewards.
    • When you press the highlighted button you get your reward and receive notification.
    • In future it will be upgraded to weekly and monthly rewards and also mission accomplishment rewards.

    Leaderboard

    It shows all players according to total kills and each nation score.

    If user selects Fire nation then leaderboard shows Fire nation score only. It will not show other nations.

    Options

    Change Background

    It changes the main menu background with a new solid attractive color.

    Apply Effects

    It turns on ant view in the main menu and shows the environment outside the wall.

    Audio

    Adjust background music, which means it is only background music wherever it will play in menus or gameplay.

    Same applies for VFx, which means sounds of buttons, effects, jumps, audio voices and all things except background music.

    Video

    These are graphics settings as per other games standards.

    At first play, graphics settings window is settled down according to your selection.

    Controls

    Keyboard and joystick controls both work in good conditions.

    For joystick mode, if you set the controls correctly, it will work.

    Basic Setting

    It is the most usable control setup including all attacks. Read and apply settings carefully.

    Shooter Setting

    It works only when you have a shooting weapon like Long Bow or Soul Reaper.

    In gameplay you need this section too, because weapons are hidden in different barrels or locations.

    Add On

    It includes swimming, zip line, push and pull and other actions which work according to the situation or scenario of the game.

    New Game Menu

    Practice Mode

    Toggle tutorial mode on to play tutorial in practice mode.

    It is recommended to play practice mode to get yourself ready for the real Antara world and its enemies.

    Bonus Mode

    Best chance to earn AQ coin and other resources to buy valuable items.

    Entry fees for one bonus round: 20 FA and 2 AQ coins.

    Be aware of evil orc and try to get all FOA coins in the shortest time.

    Random Mode

    It will drive you boss fight rounds in any scenario.

    Story Mode

    This is the real story or gameplay where every step counts and in future decides whether your Antara world is saved or not.

    Resources

    Collect resources by killing enemies to spawn enemies and to build your castle.

    Inventory (Game Play)

    On pressing the default key “I”, you can go to inventory, where you can see all your current items.

    Beside that, you can craft items in inventory and equip or unequip items too.

    Attacks

    Weak Attacks

    By default pressing left mouse button, for each weapon it is a unique weak attack.

    Strong Attacks

    By default pressing middle wheel button, similarly each weapon has a unique strong attack.

    Magic Attacks

    When your magic ball reaches the required level, you can perform magic attack and it will affect nearby enemies.

    By default magic attack key is “L”.

    Create Allies Attack

    It only works if you have 1 bone, 1 animal skin and 1 meat resource in your inventory.

    It enlarges a small wild creature on ground and makes it your ally.

    It works only in one island. It cannot swim or move forward with you.

    Gameplay Menus and Missions Objective

    Pause Menu

    On pressing Escape your game will pause and you can select different menus.

    Light Toggles

    You can set your light according to your requirements.

    Hide Canvas

    Use this if you want to record or stream your gameplay.

    Mission Panel

    To view your missions in the scenarios and check if they are clear or unclear by checking tick or not.

    Save and Load Panel

    You can save and load your mission progress.

    For saving new, you have to tick save in new slot. You can save up to 5 slots. If it is full, you can overwrite any slot.

    Return to Main Menu and Desktop

    For saving your kills, level progress and score you should press it. Do not close the game without it, otherwise no progress will be saved.

    Other menus are same as per main menu.

    Mission Objects

    This left mission panel with ant face displays all mission steps by step which you need to accomplish.

    On pressing “O” key by default this panel will toggle on and off. It will update automatically on completing each mission.

    MiniMap

    On pressing by default “M” key you can maximize or minimize minimap.

    It shows your enemies, rides, and some missions. Sometimes it will show some mission related characters.

    On maximize map you can zoom in and zoom out to see the full map or a specific area.

    Abilities

    These abilities give you super boost. It is still in progress and will be explained when launched.

    For now you can swim, which is enabled by default.
